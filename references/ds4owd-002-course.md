# Data Science for openwashdata Course (ds4owd-002, second iteration)

Source: https://ds4owd-002.github.io/website/
Captured: 2026-06-24

## Structure and duration

- 12 weeks, from 11 September to 20 November 2025, with a graduation event on 18 December.
- 9 modules delivered via Zoom, Thursdays 14:00 to 16:30 CET.

## Time commitment

- 9 live sessions of 2.5 hours each.
- About 3 hours weekly for readings and exercises.

## Tools taught

- R and the RStudio IDE.
- Git and GitHub for version control and open science collaboration.
- Tidyverse packages for data analysis.
- Quarto for reproducible document creation.

## Target audience

Professionals somewhat connected to the greater WASH sector, including public health and global health engineering. Participants must bring their own dataset to share publicly and be open to new workflows.

## Cost and access

- Offered free of charge, online, to learners of all levels.
- Sign-ups for this iteration are closed; future opportunities are announced via the openwashdata newsletter.

## Learning objectives

Master data science tools, produce reproducible documents with professional formatting, practise open science principles, and build a portfolio through a real-world capstone project.
