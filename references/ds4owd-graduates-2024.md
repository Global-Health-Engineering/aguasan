# Data Science for openwashdata: 2024 Graduates (first iteration)

Source: https://openwashdata.org/pages/academy/graduates-2024/
Captured: 2026-06-24

## Reach (from references.md note)

- More than 200 registrations.
- 50 to 100 show-ups at the first lecture.

## Graduates and outputs

- 22 completed capstone projects across WASH topics.
- 9 projects include publicly accessible data packages on the openwashdata platform.
- 13 projects maintain private GitHub repositories.
- Geographic focus across Sub-Saharan Africa, Latin America, and Asia: Colombia, Brazil, Peru, Kenya, Uganda, Malawi, Ghana, Côte d'Ivoire, South Africa, and ASEAN countries.

## Themes covered

Groundwater management, school water access, borehole functionality, urban water costs, sanitation facility management, and water service equity relative to economic indicators.

Each capstone required a defined list of deliverable items, emphasising structured, reproducible research outputs.
