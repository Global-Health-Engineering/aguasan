# Data Science for openwashdata: 2026 Graduates (second iteration)

Source: https://openwashdata.org/pages/academy/graduates-2026/
Captured: 2026-06-24

## Reach (from references.md note)

- More than 150 registrations.
- 50 to 80 show-ups per lecture.

## Graduates and outputs

- 48 individuals completed capstone projects and graduated from the 2025-2026 edition.
- Each graduate produced a capstone project with a dataset of their choice, meeting a defined list of required items.
- Topics covered water quality, sanitation services, faecal sludge management, disease surveillance, and environmental monitoring across African, Asian, and other regions.

## Availability

Most projects are publicly accessible via GitHub Pages. Not all are public; some repositories are set to private without a public website.
