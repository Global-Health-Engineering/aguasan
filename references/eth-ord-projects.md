## Summary

Between 2023 and 2026, the Global Health Engineering group (ETH Zurich) received funding from the **Open Research Data (ORD) Program of the ETH Board** across **five separate but related projects** (Explore and Contribute calls, together worth roughly CHF 500,000). Four sit in the openwashdata family of WASH data work; one, FAIRqual, focuses on qualitative research data. The funding statement on the openwashdata site reads:

> This project was supported by the Open Research Data Program of the ETH Board.

AGUASAN 2027 can provide the bridge to continue these efforts beyond the ORD funding window, turning a time-limited research-data programme into ongoing sector practice.

### 1. openwashdata - Open WASH data by building Open Science Competencies and Community

- `eth-ord-explore-01`, ORD Explore (1st call), from March 2023, CHF 150,000, 18 months.
- Built infrastructure and community, taught WASH professionals the basics of data science, and developed a FAIR data publishing workflow. Five work packages: infrastructure design, community mobilisation, data sharing protocols, data cleaning competencies, and data publishing.

### 2. openwashdata - establishing Data Stewards and increasing FAIRness

- `eth-ord-explore-02`, ORD Explore (2nd call), proposal submitted 29 February 2024.
- Repos: `openwashdata-dev/prop-eth-ord-explore-02-openwashdata` (proposal, public) and `openwashdata-dev/report-eth-ord-explore-02-openwashdata` (report, private).
- Places funded data stewards in partner organisations (UKZN WASH R&D Centre, South Africa; BASEflow, Malawi), introduces a 12-module data stewardship training programme, and strengthens FAIRness and community governance. Builds on Phase 1.

### 3. openjmp - Open JMP: unlocking the potential of global indicator data

- `eth-ord-contribute-03`, ORD Contribute, project start January 2023, CHF 30,000, 15 months.
- In partnership with the WHO/UNICEF Joint Monitoring Programme (JMP). Makes JMP input data readily accessible to researchers and practitioners, and develops open-source software for transparent documentation of modelling activities. Transforms decades of manually structured WASH coverage data from proprietary spreadsheets into documented R packages.

### 4. washcollab - Enhancing Global WASH Data Accessibility through Collaborative Initiatives

- `eth-ord-contribute-07`, ORD Contribute.
- Repos: `openwashdata-dev/prop-eth-ord-contribute-washweb` (proposal, public) and `openwashdata-dev/report-eth-ord-contribute-07-washcollab` (report, private).
- A partnership between **WASHWeb** (a practice-oriented WASH data community, author of the ORD WHO/UNICEF JMP R package) and **openwashdata**, connected through a lead JMP statistician. Maintains and develops open-source tools for analysing key WASH data from authoritative sources (WHO/UNICEF JMP, World Bank, UN-Water GLAAS, OECD), works with data providers, and disseminates through webinars and conference events. The aim is to break down siloed, hard-to-reuse WASH data and involve smaller organisations and communities.

### 5. FAIRqual - FAIR Data Practices for Qualitative Research in Transdisciplinarity

- ORD Explore (Measure 1), October 2024 to October 2025. Lives in its own org: https://github.com/fairqual (proposal: `Global-Health-Engineering/prop-eth-ord-explore-tdlab`).
- **Not part of openwashdata.** FAIRqual focuses on qualitative research data: how interviews, workshops, and other qualitative materials, which are difficult or impossible to anonymise, can be shared following FAIR principles while protecting research subjects, especially in transdisciplinary (Td) research. It prototypes tools and develops standards and guidelines for Td researchers. Example output: `fairqual/dataitd24`, digitized and tabularised flipchart data, workshop summaries, and a coding book from the FAIRqual ITD24 workshop.
- Team spans TdLab and Global Health Engineering at ETH Zurich (PD Dr. Bianca Vienni Baptista, Dr. Mollie Chapman, Dr. Franziska Mohr, Prof. Dr. Elizabeth Tilley, Lars Schöbitz).
- **Why it matters for AGUASAN:** it demonstrates direct experience with data in qualitative research, not only quantitative datasets. This positions the workshop to welcome qualitative researchers, who otherwise often feel that "open data" does not apply to their work.

## National Prize for Open Research Data 2024

The openwashdata project, led by Prof. Elizabeth Tilley (Global Health Engineering, ETH Zurich), received the National Prize for Open Research Data 2024 from the Swiss Academies of Arts and Sciences. This is recognition rather than a separate grant.
