# openwashdata: Vision, Mission, Objectives, Strategies, Tactics (VMOST)

Source: https://openwashdata.org/pages/gallery/vmost/
Captured: 2026-06-24

## Vision

> An active global community that applies FAIR principles to data generated in the greater water, sanitation, and hygiene sector.

## Mission

> Empower WASH professionals to engage with tools and workflows for open data and code.

## Objectives

1. Publish 20 R data packages on the website.
2. Receive 50 donated datasets for publication.
3. Support 5 people to independently donate, clean, and publish data.
4. Achieve 10 unique website visitors daily.
5. Reach 50% global country coverage among website visitors.
6. Achieve 2 data uses per dataset on average.
7. Grow the newsletter to 250 subscribers from 50 countries.
8. Average 5 participants per live coding event.

## Strategies

- Maintain a data warehouse showcasing published datasets.
- Develop community participation and FAIR data publishing guides.
- Build a cohort proficient in R, Git, and GitHub.
- Use Quarto and R for all communication materials.
- Promote open data adoption in the WASH sector.
- Release workshops as open educational resources.
- Introduce open science, open source, and open government concepts.
- Design a consistent corporate identity and accessibility standards.
- Frame openwashdata as a community, not merely a project.

## Tactics

- Deliver a 10-week online workshop on FAIR data publishing.
- Publish monthly blog posts on datasets, stories, and workflows.
- Issue monthly newsletters.
- Host quarterly community meetups with invited speakers.
- Create interactive dashboards, maps, and articles visualizing data.
