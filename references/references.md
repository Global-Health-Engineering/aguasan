## ETH ORD projects

- [ETH ORD Program projects](eth-ord-projects.md) - captured details and sources for all five projects.
- Project reports and repos live at https://github.com/openwashdata-dev (proposals public, reports private).
- Between 2023 and 2026, the Global Health Engineering group received Open Research Data Program funding from the ETH Board across five separate but related projects: openwashdata (explore-01, explore-02), openjmp (contribute-03), washcollab (contribute-07), and fairqual (explore, TdLab).
- Four sit in the openwashdata / WASH data family; **fairqual** focuses on data in qualitative research (its own org: https://github.com/fairqual), demonstrating experience that would help the workshop welcome qualitative researchers too.
- AGUASAN can provide the bridge to continue these efforts beyond the ORD funding window.
