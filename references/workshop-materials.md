# Open educational workshop materials

Existing, openly licensed teaching material that the AGUASAN workshop's live sessions could reuse directly. All are authored by Lars Schöbitz (Global Health Engineering, ETH Zurich) and built with Quarto.

Captured: 2026-06-24

## Git for Scientists

- Site: https://gitforsci-cis.github.io/website/
- Repo: https://github.com/gitforsci-cis/website
- Teaches Git and GitHub fundamentals for scientific collaboration: repository creation, cloning, branching, pull requests, and resolving merge conflicts using RStudio.
- Audience: researchers with prior R/RStudio experience.
- Format: a 2-hour in-person workshop (run 27 May 2025 at the ETH Zurich Center for Comparative and International Studies PhD retreat), with multi-week pre-course preparation tasks.
- Open educational resource, hosted on GitHub.

## Quarto - Authoring and Publishing Tools for Collaborative Scientific Writing

- Site: https://quarto-rdmss-26.github.io/website/
- Repo: https://github.com/quarto-rdmss-26/website
- Teaches the Quarto format and the RStudio visual editor to create scholarly documents (footnotes, cross-references, figures, tables) and to publish websites for sharing research.
- Audience: researchers and academics interested in scientific writing and digital publishing.
- Format: an in-person workshop (run 4 June 2026 at the ETH Research Data Management Summer School 2026), structured in five modules.
- Licensed CC BY 4.0; adapted from a workshop by Mine Çetinkaya-Rundel.

## AI agents for scientists

- Org: https://github.com/agentsforsci (website repo: https://github.com/agentsforsci/website)
- Material on using AI agents and agentic coding tools in a scientific workflow. Early stage: the GitHub organisation exists but no public course website is published yet (as of June 2026).
- Intended to provide the "first hands-on taste of agentic coding" the concept note describes.

## Why these matter for the proposal

The workshop does not need its curriculum built from scratch. The Git, Quarto, and (forthcoming) AI-agents material already exists as open educational resources, which lowers preparation effort and means participants leave with a stable, citable set of resources they can return to and share within their organisations.
